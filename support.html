Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from parents and students regarding Winners Grammar School & Academy.

We operate a dual educational structure: a comprehensive Morning School System for foundational grades, and afternoon/evening Academy Coaching for Class 9, 10, 11, and 12 in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics. Additionally, we provide specialized Cadet College Preparation, English Language Programs, Computer Education (DIT & CIT), and Calligraphy classes.
Our programs are structured into focused, disciplined cohorts with ideal teacher-to-student ratios. This ensures personalized academic attention, an atmosphere of dignity and focus, and structured guidance for every learner.
The DIT diploma has a planned standard duration of 6 months (subject to administrative confirmation), providing hands-on training in computing, operating systems, and office automation software.
You can visit our campus on Petroleum Road Campus during administrative hours (Morning: 7:45 AM – 1:30 PM, Evening: 2:30 PM – 7:30 PM), or message our admissions counselors via our official WhatsApp lines.
Our modern Computer Science curriculum is currently marked as Coming Soon and will be launched with specialized laboratory infrastructure in upcoming academic sessions.
